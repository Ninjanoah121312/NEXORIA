NEXORIA — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information NEXORIA may collect, why it is used, and how it is handled when you use the NEXORIA Discord bot or dashboard.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, NEXORIA may process or store information such as:

  • Discord server IDs.
  • Discord user IDs.
  • Discord role and permission information required for bot functionality.
  • Server configuration and settings.
  • Custom commands created through NEXORIA.
  • Ticket information and ticket configuration.
  • Server logging configuration.
  • Dashboard account or session information.
  • Information provided when contacting NEXORIA for support.

NEXORIA does not intentionally collect information that is not required for its features.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate NEXORIA's features.
  • Save server settings and configurations.
  • Provide ticket and support functionality.
  • Provide server logging features.
  • Operate the NEXORIA dashboard.
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Improve and develop NEXORIA.

3. Data Sharing

NEXORIA does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services when necessary to operate parts of NEXORIA, such as Discord or services used to host and operate the bot and dashboard.

4. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as it is necessary to provide NEXORIA's features or for legitimate operational and security purposes.

Some server configuration may remain stored after the bot is removed from a server unless it is deleted through an available deletion process or otherwise removed as part of normal data management.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding personal information associated with you, including requesting access to or deletion of certain information.

To request assistance with your data, contact us through the official NEXORIA Discord server.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by NEXORIA. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

NEXORIA is a Discord bot and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Users should follow Discord's applicable age requirements and policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as NEXORIA changes or new features are introduced. The latest version will be published through NEXORIA's official channels.
