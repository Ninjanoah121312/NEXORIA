Privacy Policy

NEXORIA data handling · Last updated October 4, 2026

This policy explains what information NEXORIA may process when you use the NEXORIA website, dashboard, Discord bot, integrations, modules, support features, and announcement system. The exact information processed depends on the features enabled by you or a server administrator.

1. Information NEXORIA may process

Depending on the features in use, NEXORIA may process Discord user and server identifiers, usernames, display names, avatars, roles and permission information; server configuration; module settings; moderation and automation records; logging and audit information; tickets, ticket messages and transcripts; ratings and support-review information; custom commands and variables; webhook information; reaction-role and member-automation configuration; verification information; linked Roblox account information; connected email information; and technical information required to operate the dashboard and bot.

2. Messages, tickets, logs and attachments

Features such as logging, tickets, transcripts, moderation, announcements and webhooks can process message content, message metadata and files when those features are enabled or when users submit that content. Server administrators control many of these features and are responsible for configuring them appropriately. NEXORIA may store records locally in the bot's configured data storage and may retain them for as long as they are needed for the configured feature or operational purposes.

3. Announcement attachments

Official NEXORIA announcements may include file attachments. Before Discord delivery, selected announcement files are compressed into a ZIP archive. The final compressed archive is limited to 20 MB, while the selected uncompressed files may total up to 50 MB. The compressed archive is delivered with the Discord announcement; announcement email and website announcement views may not contain the file attachment itself.

4. Connected accounts and verification

Discord, Roblox and email integrations process the information required to authenticate and provide their respective features. Roblox verification can associate a Discord identity with a Roblox account selected by the user. Email features can use a verified Main email for account recovery and NEXORIA announcements. Secondary connected email addresses are not used for announcement delivery when a verified Main email is available.

5. Local and operational data

NEXORIA can maintain server-specific data and operational state on the configured host. This can include configuration, logs, module state, runtime information and other records required to operate the bot. Data may also be exchanged between configured NEXORIA hosts when the deployment or failover architecture requires synchronization.

6. Third-party services

NEXORIA can integrate with Discord, Roblox, email delivery providers, GitHub Pages or other hosting infrastructure, Cloudflare tunnels, and other services required by enabled features. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms. NEXORIA does not control how an external service processes information once that information is provided to it.

7. Your choices and administrator controls

You can choose which connected accounts and available features to use. Server owners and administrators can enable, disable and configure many NEXORIA modules, including logging, tickets, verification, announcements and automations. Because server administrators can configure data-processing features, their own policies and legal obligations may also apply to information processed within their server.

8. Security and retention

NEXORIA uses reasonable technical safeguards appropriate to the configured environment, but no network, software or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Retention depends on the feature, server configuration, local storage, operational requirements and applicable obligations. If you need a particular record removed or have a privacy question, contact the official NEXORIA support community.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when NEXORIA adds features, changes data handling or needs to reflect legal or operational requirements. The date above identifies the currently published version.
