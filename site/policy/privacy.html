Privacy Policy

NEXORIA data handling · Last updated October 2, 2026

This policy explains what information NEXORIA may use when you use the website, Discord bot, dashboard, and enabled modules. The information used depends on the features you choose to use.

1. Information NEXORIA may use

Depending on the features you use, NEXORIA may process Discord identifiers and profile information, server configuration, tickets and transcripts, logging records, incident history, custom commands, variables, Roblox account information used for verification, and connected email information used for account features and announcements.

2. Server and module data

Module data is associated with the relevant NEXORIA server and is used to provide the features you configure. Removing the bot from a server does not necessarily remove previously retained configuration or records.

3. Connected accounts

You can manage connected Discord, Roblox, and email accounts from the available User Settings controls. For email announcements, NEXORIA uses only your verified Main email when one is selected; secondary connected emails are not used for announcement delivery.

4. Verification privacy

Roblox verification is server-scoped. You choose the Roblox account to share for a verification flow, and the available privacy controls let you manage whether your Roblox username is shown to the server.

5. Your choices

You can choose which NEXORIA features to enable and which connected accounts to use. For questions about retained data or privacy, contact the official NEXORIA support community.

6. Third parties

Discord, Roblox, email providers, and other services integrated with NEXORIA have their own privacy policies and terms. Their handling of information is governed by those services when you interact with them.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated when NEXORIA's data handling changes. The date above identifies the currently published version.
