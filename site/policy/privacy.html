NEXORIA — Privacy Policy

How NEXORIA stores and uses information needed to operate its features.

Stored information

NEXORIA may store bot configuration, module settings, tickets, logging data, incident history, connected profile information, and other data required by enabled features.

Connected accounts

When you connect an account through an OAuth provider, NEXORIA stores the minimum profile and authorization information needed for the connected feature. Disconnecting an account removes the active connection while historical records may remain where needed for the feature to work.

Credentials

Bot tokens, OAuth client secrets, API keys, and other private credentials are intended to remain on the bot host and must not be published to the website.

Questions and requests

For questions about stored data or privacy, contact the NEXORIA support team through the official support server.
